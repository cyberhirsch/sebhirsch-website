### The Project

**Blasteroids** is *Asteroids* with a second ship on the same keyboard. Each
player starts with six lives; destroying asteroids scores points, and the glowing
ones drop temporary boost power-ups worth diverting for.

![The Blasteroids title screen](featured.png)

Local multiplayer shares one keyboard — WASD and space for player one, arrow keys
and enter for player two — which is the whole point: it is built for two people
on one couch, not for matchmaking. Six save slots let a run be parked and resumed
later, and the pause menu is a keypress away at any time.
