### The Project

An interactive tracker for both *Conan Exiles* maps — the Exiled Lands and the
Isle of Siptah — holding roughly **4,500 catalogued points** across a deep
category tree, with per-category visibility so the map stays readable.

![The Isle of Siptah tracker](featured.png)

Markers span discovery locations (bosses, chests, lore, recipes, emotes, caves,
vistas), named and unnamed thralls by profession, resource nodes down to
individual silver and star metal deposits, tameable pets, and harvestable plants.
Custom categories can be added on top for a player's own annotations — enemy
bases, map rooms, personal waypoints.

The point of building it locally rather than using one of the online map sites
was progress state: what has actually been visited, cleared, or harvested is
personal data, and it belongs in the browser rather than on someone else's
server.
