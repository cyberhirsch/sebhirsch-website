### The Project

**TypeGrid** treats letterforms like a modular synth: toggle segments on a grid and an entire alphabet updates in step. It supports rectangular, curvilinear, and hexagonal grid systems, turning type design into rapid, systematic experimentation rather than one-off drawing.
